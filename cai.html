01 / The route

Class progression

Work through these four stages in order. Use the status menu on each card to track your run.

02 / Skill priorities

Training order

Spear D→Spear C→Sword B→Spear A + Sword A

Build Spear first while raising Riding from E+ to D. Keep training Riding to C as Sword approaches B for Bardinger. For Orichaldia, target Spear A, Sword A, and Riding B.

!

Check the Light Cavalry exam. Sources differ on whether it requires both C-rank weapons or allows either one. Use the requirements shown in your game.

03 / On the battlefield

How to play the build

↗

Combat & positioning

Use Cai's mounted reach to initiate on priority enemies and reposition. Spears are the main damage investment. His innate Brio adds +1 movement.

⚔

Equipment

Carry the best reliable spear you have, plus a high-might spear for tougher foes. Keep a sword for useful matchups or certification training, and prioritize a useful mount.

04 / Reference

Source & verification
