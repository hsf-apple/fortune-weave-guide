Privacy & backups

Updated 8 November 2026

Fortune’s Weave Build Companion is a browser-based game planner. This page describes how the companion stores your progress and uses optional Google Drive access.

Progress in your browser

Your rosters, class plans, ranks, notes, tasks, loadouts, quest progress, and spoiler settings are saved in this browser’s local storage. Clearing site data removes that browser save. An exported or previously saved Drive backup is required to restore it.

Downloaded backups

Export creates a JSON file on your device. Import reads the file you choose and asks for confirmation before replacing your browser save. Anyone with a copy of the JSON file can read its contents; keep it somewhere you trust.

Optional Google Drive access

When Drive backups are enabled on this site, the companion loads Google’s Identity Services library. Connecting opens Google’s account and authorization window. The companion requests only drive.appdata, which permits access to this app’s private Drive data folder. It does not request access to your other Drive files, Gmail, or contacts.

Save to Google Drive (or Sync in the header) uploads a dated JSON backup of all four paths directly from your browser to your selected Google account. Each click creates a separate backup and leaves earlier backups intact. Load from Google Drive lists backups in that account and downloads the selected backup for a preview. Your browser save is replaced only after you confirm Restore. Normal edits do not trigger Drive uploads or downloads.

The companion uses Google data only to provide the backup and restore features you request. It does not send your saves or access tokens to a developer-operated server, sell them, use them for advertising, or use them to train AI models. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Connection and retention

Google access tokens stay in memory within the current browser tab, including while you navigate around the companion. They are not stored in browser storage or backup files. Reloading or closing the tab discards the token, and Google access also expires. A small “used Drive before” preference is saved locally so the app can offer Save and Load on later visits; it contains no Google token or account details. When access is needed again, clicking Save, Load, or Reconnect opens Google authorization. Save and Load continue the requested action after authorization succeeds. Switch account opens Google’s account chooser. Disconnect clears the current token and remembered preference without deleting backups or revoking Google’s authorization.

Drive backups remain in your Google account until you remove them. They are hidden from the normal Drive file list. You can manage this app’s data through Google Drive Settings → Manage apps, and revoke authorization through Google Account connections. Revoking authorization alone does not remove existing backups. Deleting all app data removes your Drive backup history. Downloaded copies and browser saves must be removed separately.

Hosting and third parties

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses as described in GitHub’s privacy statement. When you use Google features, Google processes connection and authorization information according to Google’s privacy policy.

Contact

Contact the maintainer through the project repository. Avoid posting your backup files or personal notes in public issues.
